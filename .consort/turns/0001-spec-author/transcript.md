## Final reasoning

I wrote S1-file-stock's three acceptance criteria and the `feature-spec.md` narrative. They use exactly the AC ids in `registration.json`, and the S1 self-check passed.

Choosing an existing location or entering a new one has no AC of its own, since the registered set has no slot for it. AC1 covers it. Under `## Open questions` I left three calls for you, each with my recommended answer:
- **Re-filing the same (sku, location):** update the existing record rather than reject it. AC3 is written this way.
- **Tracking code:** optional when filing. Views show "not tracked" when it's missing.
- **Quantity:** zero is allowed and negative numbers are rejected on the form.
